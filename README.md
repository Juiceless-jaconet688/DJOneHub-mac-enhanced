The application manages most tasks automatically. Connect your 4G module to a USB port on your Mac. The software detects the device once you open the dashboard.

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

**Will this work if I disconnect my 4G module?**
The software requires a constant USB connection to the hardware module to display real-time alerts. If you unplug the module, the alerts stop until you reconnect the device.

**Can I answer calls directly on my Mac?**
Currently, the software supports incoming call alerts and the ability to decline calls. Full audio support remains in development.

**Is my SIM card safe?**
Yes. The software only reads the data needed for notifications and connection management. It does not modify, delete, or send data from your SIM card without your direct action.

**Why do I see a security prompt when I open the app?**
Your Mac shows this prompt because the app comes from a developer outside the App Store. Right-click the app icon, choose "Open," and select "Open" again in the warning box to run it.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

*   **Notifications not appearing:** Open System Settings on your Mac. Go to Notifications and verify that DJOneHub has permission to send alerts.
*   **No device found:** Unplug the USB cable and plug it into a different port. Ensure the 4G module has a blinking light to show it has power.
*   **Browser panel keeps closing:** Click the "Stay Active" button on the dashboard to prevent the background processes from sleeping.
